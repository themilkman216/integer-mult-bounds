## Findings

### 1. Counting bound: shipped fills are 0.030283 above the pattern optimum; the entire gap is 150 cap-16 `[9]` bins

Both shipped fills use only 11 bin patterns (`ANALYSIS.json`): cap-8 all `[8]`; cap-16 `[7,9]`/`[8,8]` plus 150 `[9]` (rem 7); cap-32 `[4,9,9,9]` (rem 1, 23976), `[5,9,9,9]`, `[7,8,8,9]`, `[7,7,9,9]`, `[9,9,9]` (rem 5); cap-48 `[9,9,9,9,9]` (rem 3, 8673) and 24479 empty. Minimising idle moment over pattern counts (bin counts per cap + item counts per width fixed; windows/families ignored) gives an **integral** LP optimum (`scripts/patterns.py`, `scripts/lp_primal.py`; `PATTERN-LP.json`, `LP-PRIMAL.json`) beating both shipped fills by the same amount. Exact rational dual certificate (`scripts/bounds.py`, `BOUNDS.json`, re-derived in `verify.py`): idle moment of *any* fill is at least `16866.80536066968` (43) / `17095.928928708723` (48), versus shipped `16866.835643858758` / `17095.95921189753` — gaps `0.030283189074074682` / `0.030283188809198038`. Reduced-cost decomposition (exact, `BOUNDS.json`): every shipped pattern except cap-16 `[9]` is tight (11 tight patterns incl. cap-32 `[8,8,8,8]`, unused by shipped fills); `rc([9]16) = 0.0002018875834714359`, and `150 × rc` is the whole gap. Over the 12 patterns {11 tight + `[9]`}, the balance equations force the `[9]`-count `k` to satisfy `x[8×4]+x[7,8,8,9]+x[7,7,9,9] = 2294 + k/2` and, for 43, `x[9×5]48 = 8658 + k/10` — hence `10 | k`. The minimal improving exchange is therefore the 16-bin unit `10×[9]16 + 5×[7,7,9,9]32 + 1×[9×5]48 → 10×[7,9]16 + 5×[9,9,9]32 + 1×[]48`, with exact idle change `Δ = f(48)+5f(5)−10f(7)−f(3)`, certified `Δ_hi = −0.00201887583198831` at `A2269` and `−0.0020154436918508713` at endpoint `b` (both strictly negative). For 48 vs 43 the pattern optima differ only by `3330×([5,9,9,9]→[9,9,9])`, i.e. `+3330 f(5)` idle, exactly cancelling the paid saving — so at counting level the 48-alias total optimum equals the 43 total optimum, and round 3C's invariance is the `k=150` special case of the same identity, not a counting-level law at `k<150`.

### Interim findings (float/discovery unless stated exact; scripts to be listed under Tests run)

1. **Family distinctness collapses to per-bin distinct calls (exact integer fact, to be rechecked in the verifier).** Windows are the full grid `rep in 0..73 x child in 0..17` (1332 windows); cap and `Q(child)=2*pairs+sides` depend only on `child`; every window's allowed time set is a contiguous interval. Family `fam = G*112+port` with `G=rep*8+Q(child)` (592 groups). Windows sharing a group `G` (same `rep`, same `Q`; 1036 pairs) have **pairwise disjoint** time intervals (0 overlaps, direct check). Hence two items of one call share a family iff they lie in the same bin: same-window different bins differ by port, same-`G` different windows cannot both contain that call, different `G` differ outright. Port assignment is therefore free (any bijection per window); a from-scratch solver needs only "no bin contains two items of one call", not port-swap repair.
